Fixes the app sending your whole database to the server every now and then.

When the app fetched a change made on another device and saved it into your .kdbx file, the library it writes the file with quietly gave every entry, group and deletion a new modification time. On the next sync everything looked changed, so the app sent all of it again: several hundred items at once, about once a week. Nothing was lost, because the contents were the same, but each round pushed real older versions out of the server's version history, which keeps only the last three.

The app now keeps the original times when it writes the file, and only real changes are sent.
